# Privacy Policy for Verto

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Developer:** Moheyeldin Emad  
**Contact:** contact.meldin@gmail.com

Verto ("the App") is a unit, global currency, and precious metals (Gold & Silver) converter built with privacy by design. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Verto.

---

## 1. Information We Access & How It Is Used

### Approximate Location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) — Optional
- **Purpose:** When you grant approximate location permission, Verto uses Android's on-device location and geocoding services solely to determine your country code (for example, `US`, `EG`, `GB`, `SA`) so it can automatically pre-select your default unit system (Metric / US Imperial / UK), default target currency, and local Gold & Silver market prices.
- **On-Device Processing Only:** Your coordinates are processed locally on your device. Verto does **not** send, upload, or store your GPS/network coordinates on any developer-owned server.
- **Full User Control:** Location access is completely optional. You can deny the permission or override your measurement system, currency pair, and country market manually at any time in **Settings**.

### Network Access (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)
- Verto connects to public market-data APIs (such as Open Exchange Rates, Frankfurter, Yahoo Finance, and Stooq) to fetch live currency exchange rates, Gold (`XAU`) / Silver (`XAG`) spot prices, and historical chart series up to 5 years.
- Standard HTTP requests made by your device to these public endpoints do not include personal identifiers, user accounts, or advertising IDs from the App.

### Local Storage (On-Device Only)
- Your preferences (language, theme, navigation bar position, start screen, preferred units/currencies) and cached market rates/charts for offline mode are stored locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing its app data removes all stored preferences and cached data.

## 2. Data Collection, Sharing & Monetization
- **No Accounts Required:** Verto does not require sign-in or registration.
- **No Analytics or Advertising SDKs:** Verto contains no ads and uses no third-party analytics, crash-tracking, or profiling SDKs.
- **No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data:** We do not collect, sell, rent, or trade personal user data.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Verto does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
